About

I'm Dilan Abeynarayana. I'm Australian. I've been teaching since 2009 and working in IB international schools since 2013: Discovery College, Hong Kong Academy, Beijing BISS, and now SWIS in Shenzhen. I'm currently the Subject Lead for a three‑language L&L department (Chinese, English, Spanish) and finishing my Master of Education.

I've been an IB examiner since 2016 and a Team Leader since 2018, overseeing Paper 1, the HL Essay, and formerly the Written Tasks. Over those years I've marked thousands of essays and seen the same problems repeat. Most IB English teachers know the default approaches aren't working: students label techniques instead of analysing, feedback doesn't stick, units collapse when the text changes.

I built a set of systems that fix this. I call it Lazy Efficiency. This site collects every resource I've made: unit planning tools, annotation rubrics, deconstruction frames, department audit checklists, conceptual unit planners. All free.

When I'm not teaching or marking, I'm probably playing the drums badly, coaching touch rugby, or trying to finish my thesis. Subscribe to the YouTube channel for the full walkthroughs.
