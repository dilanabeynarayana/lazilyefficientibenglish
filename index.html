Nothing Is Standalone

Every resource links to the others. The annotation rubric feeds the TEEAL paragraph. The Stages of IDEAs feed the elaboration, which feeds your feedback. In class they become shorthand — "What stage is that idea? Is that a MUSE‑level deconstruction?" — and over time they spread across year levels and subjects until your whole department speaks the same language.
