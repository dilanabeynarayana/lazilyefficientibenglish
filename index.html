The Lazily Efficient IB English Teacher

Biggest impact. Fewest moving parts. Everything your students need to stop describing and start adding — implications, connotations, and real arguments.

Every IB Response Needs To Do These Three Things

1. Add Implications

Break ideas into stages. Move from "friendship" to exactly what kind, why it matters, and what the writer is claiming.

2. Add Connotations

Deconstruct specific words, visuals, and elements — then link them to a precise idea. No more "vivid imagery."

3. Add an Argument

A real thesis, not "this essay will explore." A claim that only exists because the student built it.
